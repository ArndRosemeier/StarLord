# Auto build for a single planet

- The production queue of every colony now offers an **Auto build** item. Pick a production strategy for it (Balanced, Expansion, Industry, Research or Military), and whenever it comes up first in the queue it puts what that strategy picks for this planet in front of itself, then waits behind it for the next turn it comes up. It stays in the queue until you remove it.
- The queue row shows what it would pick right now, and its tooltip shows the cost and how many turns that takes at the colony's industry.
- When the strategy has nothing worth building there, Auto build says it is idle and the items behind it are built instead.
- A colony with Auto build in its queue is left to it by production automation.
