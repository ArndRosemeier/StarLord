# Battle damage stays
- Ships keep their armor and structure damage after a battle; the fleet panel shows how many are damaged.
- A ship that spends a turn at a system with one of your colonies or outposts is fully repaired. In deep space or at foreign stars it stays damaged.
- Advanced Damage Control, a cybernetic race or an Engineer captain repair every ship fully after each battle; an Automated Repair Unit does it for its own ship.
- Damaged ships start their next battle with less armor, and the battle replay shows it.
