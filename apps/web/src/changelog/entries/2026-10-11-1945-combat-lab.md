# Combat lab

- The debug menu has a combat lab: set up two fleets by hull, weapons, count and tech level, give the defender an armed planet, a planet shield and a Star Base, Battlestation or Star Fortress, then watch the battle in 3D.
- Presets cover fleet battles, missiles against beams, fortified planets, Star Fortress sieges and doom stars.
- Fight a setup 100 times to see win rates and average losses, or test 200 random battles. Every battle is checked for broken combat rules.
- Fix: two empires at the same star now fight one battle per turn. A battle that ran out of time used to start again right away, up to six times in one turn.
- Fix: a fleet that is not on attack stance now shoots back at a planet's guns firing on it, instead of taking fire for the whole battle.
