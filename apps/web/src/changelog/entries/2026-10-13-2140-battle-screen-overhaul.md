# New battle screen
- Commanded battles now fill the whole screen. Your units are listed on the left and the enemy's on the right, each with a health bar. An enemy's tag shows your best chance to hit it, and clicking it makes it your target.
- The deck at the bottom has three parts. The unit on its turn shows armor, structure, shields per facing and movement left. Each weapon group is a tile with its chance to hit and expected damage; tap the tile to switch it between Fire, Standby and Off. The target shows its range and its health.
- The Fire button says how much damage should get through the target's shields. The target card has buttons to step through targets.
- Keys: F fire, M move, W wait, D done, S scan, Tab next target, Esc cancel.
- On tablets the unit lists open with Forces, and the deck stacks to fit the screen.
- The 3D battle camera now keeps every ship in view at the start.
