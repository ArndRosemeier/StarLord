# Star systems in 3D
- Click a star on the galaxy map to open its system up close in a 3D view: the star, every planet on its orbit, asteroid belts, ringed gas giants, colonies in their owner's colours, star bases and the fleets parked there.
- Drag to turn the view and scroll to zoom. Click a planet (in the view or in the list beside it) and the camera glides over and follows it; double-click to see the whole system again.
- Colonize, invade and pick fleets from the panel beside the view. Picking a fleet closes the view so you can send it off.
- With a fleet selected, clicking a star still sends the fleet there as before.
