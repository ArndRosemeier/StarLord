# Strike the turn you arrive

- Warships arriving at an enemy system now ask what to attack right away, as in the original, so the battle is fought the turn they arrive instead of one turn later. "Attack nothing" keeps them on Defend.
- After a won orbit, the orbit menu now offers Invade: every troop ship in orbit lands at once, even on the arrival turn.
- You can order Invade while a fleet is still on its way; it lands its troops the turn it arrives and wins the orbit.
- Telepaths are asked about Mind control the turn they arrive, whatever their fleet's stance.
- Computer empires also land their troops the turn they win the orbit.
