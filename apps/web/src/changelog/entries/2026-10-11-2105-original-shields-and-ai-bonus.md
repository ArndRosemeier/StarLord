# Shields and computer bonuses as in the original

- Shields recover as in the original: each combat turn a ship regains 30% of one facing's strength, shared a point at a time over its weakened facings (all of it with Shield Capacitors). Before, every facing regained 30%, so a ship hit from several sides recovered up to four times as much.
- Computer empires' difficulty bonus is now the original's flat extra per working unit: −½ (Tutor), 0 (Easy), +½ (Average), +1 (Hard) and +2 (Impossible) industry or research per worker and scientist, half that in food per farmer. Before it was a percentage, which made Average to Impossible computers two to three times stronger than in the original.
- Fix: a new research treaty can no longer push research progress below zero.
