# Breakthroughs are celebrated

- A research breakthrough now opens a festive window with fireworks in the field's colour and a short fanfare. There are four looks and four fanfares, so it does not get old.
- The window shows what you just learned with its real numbers: damage, space, cost, and the buildings and bonuses it unlocks.
- Closing it takes you straight to the Research screen to pick the next field, unless research is automated.
- Techs gained another way the same turn (spies, treaties, salvage) are listed under the breakthrough.
- The fanfare follows your music volume and mute, and the background music dips while it plays.
