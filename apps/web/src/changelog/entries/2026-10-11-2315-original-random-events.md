# Random events by the original rules

- Each kind of random event now happens at most once a game, as in the classic game. Over a long game, events slowly become rarer.
- A comet hit no longer wipes out the colony. It does (d3 + d3) × (buildings + population) / 10 hits on buildings and colonists. Every ship in orbit, armed or not and whoever owns it, wears the comet down by its hull size each turn (frigate 1 up to doom star 6).
- Earthquakes do (d3 + d2) × (buildings + population) / 10 hits. Industrial accidents kill 20 to 60% of a colony's people and no longer turn the planet radiated. Depletion only strikes ultra rich worlds.
- Pirates and pirate raids are now two separate events. A raid takes 30 to 50% of a treasury of at least 100 BC. Pirates only come to empires with 4 or more freighters. Their base is as strong as the fleets of the empires in the system, scaled down on easier levels. While it stands, it takes freighters and halves trade. Every ship in orbit wears it down by its hull size.
- A plague lasts until its cure is found. The cure costs the colony's research times (d8 + 2 × difficulty).
- A supernova can threaten any colonised star, home systems too. Stopping it takes the system's research for the turns it gives you. If it explodes, the system's worlds are left radiated.
- A breakthrough completes your current research at once.
- Diplomatic marriages and assassinations are full-size relation events (+100 or −100, scaled the usual way). They never happen between empires at war.
- A derelict ship teaches the beam weapon closest above the strongest any empire has, and the best armor any empire has.
- An unstable wormhole now carries a fleet to its destination at once. The hyperspace beast destroys a ship in flight every turn, choosing a new victim each time.
- Lasting events (flux, anomaly, beast, population boom) last 6 to 22 turns. The warp funnel does nothing, as in the original.
- Lucky races' bonus windfalls replace that turn's normal roll, and they only come when the draw is a windfall.
