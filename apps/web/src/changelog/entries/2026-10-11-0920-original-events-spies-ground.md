# Events, research, spies and ground combat follow the original
- Random events start on turn 50 and get likelier with every quiet turn, then the galaxy stays calm for 5 turns. Every event is equally likely; disasters strike the most populous empires and windfalls the smaller ones. Comets and supernovas wait until turn 200, and mutinies no longer happen.
- Lucky races build up to windfalls of their own instead of seeing good events twice as often.
- Research breakthroughs: once points pass a field's cost, the chance each turn is how far past it you are (1% just past, certain at twice the cost). Points past the cost are lost. Each further hyper-advanced level costs 10,000 more.
- The Galactic Council first meets once half of all star systems are owned, from turn 25, then every 25 turns.
- Spies roll once per target each turn as a team: 80 steals, 70 sabotages, 90 frames a third empire. Defending spies get the race's full spying bonus.
- Ground combat: militia fight at -10, marines at 0 and tanks at +10, plus basic armor. Defenders fight tanks first, then marines, then militia, and a tie wounds both sides. Powered Armor helps marines and Battleoids help tanks. Militia is one unit per 5 colonists.
- Conquered aliens revolt at 1% per unassimilated unit each turn.
