# Random events like the original
- Donations grow as the game goes on: 100 BC plus 100 for every 20 turns.
- A computer virus needs at least 10 stored research and wipes 51 to 100 points of it.
- Pirates now strike treasuries of 100 BC or more and take 30% to 50% of it, then choke trade until you drive them off.
- Comets are tougher and come sooner on higher difficulties; supernovas also come sooner on harder settings.
- Mineral discoveries raise a planet's richness by two steps.
- Hyperspace flux, time-space anomalies, pirates and the warp beast can fade after 5 turns and always end by 20.
- Tolerant races are never hit by industrial accidents.
