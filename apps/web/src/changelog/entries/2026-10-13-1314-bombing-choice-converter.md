# Bomb, poison or destroy: your call

- When your attacking fleets hold the orbit of an enemy colony after the battles, the turn now stops and asks what they do: bombard, bombard with biological weapons, destroy the planet, or hold fire.
- Each choice shows the colony's defenders and the expected losses in colonists and buildings, and the exact relation hit with each computer empire for biological weapons and planet destruction.
- Biological weapons are no longer released by themselves: ordinary bombing leaves them aboard, so you only take the diplomatic penalty when you choose to.
- Biological weapons now launch every bombardment round they have ammunition for, as bombs do, so they kill far more colonists than before.
- A ship with a Stellar Converter can destroy an enemy planet from orbit: it becomes an asteroid belt, and everyone and everything on it is lost. In battle the converter still only wears down the planet's defenses.
- Computer empires decide too: they spare colonies they are about to invade, use biological weapons when ruthless enough, and fit Stellar Converters on their biggest warships to destroy planets they cannot take.
- The turn report now tells you what your bombardments destroyed.
