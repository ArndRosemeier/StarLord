# Race specials checked against the original

- Ship defense racial bonus now also helps your ships dodge missiles, like the original.
- Conquered colonies are defended with their new owner's ground combat bonuses.
- Trans-dimensional fleets are slowed by nebulae like everyone else (they still ignore hyperspace flux).
- Repulsive races can only declare war or sue for peace: no gifts, demands or tech trades, and their peace offers count 50 points worse. Computer rulers no longer pile up grievances against them.
- Cybernetic colonies round their food need up, and surplus food is sold from the empire's net surplus, rounded down.
- Every race ability description now lists its real numbers (Fantastic Traders get +50% trade treaty income).
