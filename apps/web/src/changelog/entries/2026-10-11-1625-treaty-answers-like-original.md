# Computer rulers answer treaties like the original

- Computer rulers no longer accept every treaty on sight. Like in the original, each answer weighs their attitude toward you, a d100, what the treaty asks of them, their temperament and your diplomatic bonuses.
- Every computer ruler has one of six temperaments, from Xenophobic to Pacifistic; the diplomacy screen shows it.
- The audience shows your chance of acceptance before you ask, with every part of the sum.
- Asking wears down their patience: each proposal counts against the next answers, and too many asks or demands make them refuse to listen until patience recovers. Breaking a treaty exhausts it.
- An alliance needs a non-aggression pact first, and peace comes only after 10 turns of war.
