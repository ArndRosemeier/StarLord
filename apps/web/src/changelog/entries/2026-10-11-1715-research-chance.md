# Research shows its breakthrough chance

- Research works like the original: the breakthrough chance stays at 0% until your points pass the field's cost, then climbs each turn until it is certain at twice the cost.
- The research screen and the top bar now show this turn's breakthrough chance, and before that how many turns until the first chance.
- Research points made while no field is chosen are now lost, as in the original, so pick a field every turn.
