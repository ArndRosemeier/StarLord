# Prices, trust and the original peace rule

- A ruler that almost agrees to a non-aggression pact, an alliance or peace may name a price instead of refusing: BC, a technology, a tribute or, for a big price, a star system. Pay it in the audience and the treaty is signed.
- Breaking a treaty now costs the victim's trust in you and some of your reputation with every empire you know, and both count in all later treaty answers. Honorable rulers take it hardest.
- Peace follows the original: attitude no longer matters, the war does. Long wars, their other wars, your strength and your ships in their systems make peace likelier; their ships in your systems make it harder.
- The audience shows the chance of a yes and of a price, with every part of the sum.
