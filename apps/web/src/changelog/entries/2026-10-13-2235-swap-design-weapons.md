# Swap weapons in the ship designer

- Each weapon row in the ship designer now has a dropdown to swap its weapon in place, for example Neutron Blasters to Graviton Beams. The count, firing arc and modifications carry over where the new weapon allows them.
- Every option shows its damage, space and cost per weapon, and says when fewer will fit in the hull.
- A new ✕ button removes a whole weapon row at once.
