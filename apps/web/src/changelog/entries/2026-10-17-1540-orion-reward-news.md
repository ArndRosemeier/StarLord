# The Guardian's fall makes the news

- Destroying the Guardian of Orion now opens a Galactic News front page that lists exactly what you found on the elders' ship: the Death Ray and the other xenon technologies you learned, the Avenger battleship now waiting at Orion, and whether the last of the elders took command of it. Before, these rewards were granted with only small lines in the turn report.
- Every other empire reads in the news that you visited the legendary Orion system.
- The Research screen has a new Xenon technology panel that lists every xenon tech you own (Death Ray, Particle Beam and the rest). These techs are never researched, so they never appeared in the tech fields before.
- Each field's "Known" list now also shows techs you stole, traded for or salvaged in tiers you haven't finished.
