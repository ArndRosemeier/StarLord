# Colonize from the system view
- When your colony or outpost ship is in a system, its 3D view marks every planet it can settle with a green ✚ Colonize tag and shows a hint.
- Click such a planet and a Colonize button appears right under it. The planet list beside the view has the same button.
- Colonize now works with any of your colony ships at the star. You no longer have to select the fleet first, on the galaxy map too.
