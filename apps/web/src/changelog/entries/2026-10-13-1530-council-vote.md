# You vote at the Galactic Council
- The council now announces each session a turn ahead, naming the two candidates and how many votes elect a leader.
- During that turn a window asks for your votes: back either candidate or abstain, with how the computer empires are leaning and what each choice does to your relations. The Diplomacy screen shows the same ballot.
- After the vote a window shows how every empire voted, the count against the two thirds needed, and why someone was or wasn't elected. You answer an election you can refuse right there.
- Vote counts for other empires are now right everywhere: they no longer count only the colonies you have seen.
