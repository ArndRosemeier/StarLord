# Monsters and leaders as in the original

- Space monsters are now built as in the original: no armor, their own structure, beam attack from their computer and
  beam defense from their speed. They carry their original weapons, among them the wyrm's fire, the crystal's lance and
  the hydra's breath, and they fire all round.
- Monsters that invade from beyond the rim are bigger kinds of the lair monsters, and they may head for your homeworld.
- Ion eels never start a fight; they only fight back.
- While another empire is in the same system, monsters and raiders leave lucky empires alone.
- The Guardian of Orion has its original stats: 800 armor, 800 structure, a class V shield with hard shields, and its
  original weapons and specials. It is much weaker than before, but still deadly.
- Slaying the Guardian now gives you the Avenger, the battleship it guarded, with the last of the elders in command
  when a captain's post is free. It also adds a score bonus.
- In battle, the best ship officer on each side now helps every ship on that side: Weaponry gives attack, Helmsman
  gives defense (half against missiles), Ordnance gives extra damage to every weapon, and Fighter Pilot helps
  fighters. Galactic Lore and Scout Labs now help only against monsters.
- Commando officers help troops landing from orbit at a higher rate. Colony leaders help the defenders.
- Megawealth leaders pay a flat 10 BC a turn (15 improved).
- Warlike races' leaders no longer serve one level higher. Only they can reach level 6.
- Stronger leaders appear sooner in games with more empires.
