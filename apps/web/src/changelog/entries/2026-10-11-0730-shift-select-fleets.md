# Command several fleets at once
- Shift-click fleets on the galaxy map, in your fleet list, in a system's fleet list or in the 3D system view to select several at once. Shift-click a selected fleet to take it out again.
- Click a star and every selected fleet heads there. Fleets that cannot reach it stay put, and the notice tells you which.
- Hovering a star shows each selected fleet's route, and a panel lists the selection so you can inspect or drop any fleet.
