# Many smaller rules now match the original

- Troop ships carry 4 marines and can only be built at a colony with a Marine Barracks.
- Barracks hold up to half (marines) or a quarter (tanks) of the maximum population; warlike races train twice as fast and keep twice as many.
- Natives pay no tax and don't grow. Only colonies where natives live among settlers suffer the mixed-race morale penalty.
- The Galactic Currency Exchange is now a technology: +50% BC at every colony, with no building to buy.
- Extended Fuel Tanks give a fleet +50% range when every ship carries them.
- Scout Labs make research every turn: 1 to 32 points by hull size.
- Captains die with their ship unless you have Survival Pods and another of your ships survives the battle.
- Warlike races' leaders serve one level higher.
- Reflection Fields bounce beams back more often.
- Spying: assassins kill at most one enemy spy a turn, stolen technology never comes from the field you are researching, and a framed empire loses more goodwill.
