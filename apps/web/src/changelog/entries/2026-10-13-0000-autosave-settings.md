# Autosave, settings and hotkeys

- The game now saves itself at the start of every turn and when you leave the page. The newest two autosaves are kept (marked "Auto" in the saved games list), and "Continue" on the title screen picks up your newest save. If browser storage runs full, older autosaves make room; your own saves are never deleted.
- New Settings section under Game (the ⚙ button in the top bar, or G): music and effects volume, battle replay speed (½× to 4×), autosave on or off, and this game's switches for the Trade Goods warning and commanding your battles. The title screen's Settings button has the same sound, replay and autosave options.
- Battle replays have a speed button too.
- One-letter keys open the screens, as in the original: C colonies, T research, D ship design, F fleets, L leaders, R diplomacy, S espionage, I empire, E turn report, G game, M galaxy map. Esc goes back to the map.
- On the map, N jumps to your next idle fleet and . (period) to your next colony; both also have buttons above the side panel. The full list of keys is in Game › Settings. Keys do nothing while you type.
