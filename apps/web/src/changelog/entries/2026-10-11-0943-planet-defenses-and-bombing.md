# Stronger planets, original bombing

- New building: the Planetary Stellar Converter. It hits every shield of a ship in orbit for 400 each combat turn.
- Fighter Garrisons now fight: they launch interceptors, bombers or heavy fighters from the planet and send up fresh craft every 10 combat turns.
- Bombing works like the original: every 100 damage that gets past the planetary shield is one hit, landing at random on a building, a trained marine or tank, or a colonist. Orbital stations and planetary defenses can no longer be bombed away.
