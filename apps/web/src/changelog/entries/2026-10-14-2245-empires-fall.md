# Empires fall, and the galaxy hears it
- When an empire is destroyed, every ruler gets a front-page Galactic News report with its portrait, who is credited with its destruction and how many empires remain.
- Computer empires can now give up and join another empire, rarely, as in the original: from turn 150, one stuck in a long war with another computer empire that is small, has a weak fleet or has lost much of its population may surrender instead of making peace. It joins the most populous computer empire it knows and is not at war with; on Tutor, Easy and Average it may even join you.
- A surrender makes the front page too, showing both rulers and exactly what changed hands: colonies, population, ships, new technologies, BC, freighters and spies.
- Surrender now works like the original: the receiver also gets the freighters and every spy (up to 63 at home), only the technologies it can use, and the surrendering empire's leaders leave service. The final score table says who surrendered to whom.
