# Every monster looks like itself

- Monsters stay hidden until you explore their system. You find out what guards a system by flying in.
- Each monster kind has its own 3D model in the system view and in battle: the banded, crackling Ion Eel, the winged Star Wyrm, the translucent Void Amoeba, the shard-cluster Lattice Crystal, the three-headed Star Hydra and the ringed golden Guardian.
- Monster fleets are named by kind ("Ion Eel" instead of "Monsters") and say what they do: most attack every ship that enters their system, while Ion Eels never attack first and only fight back.
