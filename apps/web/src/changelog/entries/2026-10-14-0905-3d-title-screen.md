# The title screen goes 3D
- The title screen is now a living 3D scene: the game's real ship models, in every race's own design style, fly banking passes, loops, barrel rolls, dogfights and formations that drop out of warp.
- Behind them float a homeworld with its orbital station and moons, a far-off world and a distant sun, in front of one of three brand-new painted nebulae.
- No two starts look the same: the homeworld, its moons and station, the sun's colour and place, the far planet, the nebula and the races flying past are picked anew every time.
- The toys are still there: click a ship to shoot it down (pirate raiders pay a bounty), click empty space to warp a ship in, and the secret code still summons a Grand Armada.
- New game jumps into hyperspace. On tablets the scene is lighter, and with reduced motion it shows one still frame.
