# The council in session
- The Galactic Council now plays out on screen as the turn starts: every empire's delegation declares its vote in seat order, and a running count fills toward the two thirds that elect a leader.
- When your seat comes up the session waits for you: back a candidate or abstain, seeing how the empires still to declare are leaning.
- The verdict is shown as soon as the last delegation has spoken. The Diplomacy screen takes you back into the chamber until you end the turn.
