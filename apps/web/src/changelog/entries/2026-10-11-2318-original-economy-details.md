# Colony details as in the original

- New colonists now pick jobs the way the original does. They farm if their colony would run short of food, or if your empire has no food to spare. They work in industry if the colony can't cover its cybernetic or android upkeep. Otherwise they join industry or research at random, in the proportion those jobs already have. New colonies still start split evenly.
- When a colony starves, a random worker or scientist dies first. Farmers die only when no one else is left.
- Conquered people assimilate on a points scale. Uneven rates now carry over: charismatic Galactic Unification takes 7.5 turns per unit instead of 8.
- Spies cost 100 production to train, up from 25.
- Freighter upkeep is half a BC per freighter in use, rounded down for the whole fleet.
- Barracks train a marine or a tank battalion every 5 turns, up to half (marines) or a quarter (tanks) of the colony's current population. Above that share of the planet's size, one unit leaves each turn. Warlords keep twice as many troops but no longer train faster.
- Debt now sells buildings with upkeep first, the least valuable first, for half their cost instead of a quarter. Farms, factories, Biospheres, morale buildings and a colony's only barracks count as more valuable than their price.
- An empty build queue sells only that turn's production. Production you have already stored waits for your next item.
- A time-space anomaly freezes its system completely. Colonies there make, use and pay nothing and don't grow until it passes.
- An empire is eliminated when its last populated colony falls, even if it still has colony ships.
- Pirate caches hold 100 BC and ship debris 50 BC.
- Splinter colonies have 3 population, or fewer if the planet can't hold that many.
- Natives are always 3 population. Each native farmer grows 2 extra food before morale and other bonuses apply.
- Ancient artifacts reward the first empire to explore their system, not the first to settle the planet. It learns a random technology from its open research fields, with a 25% chance of a second one.
