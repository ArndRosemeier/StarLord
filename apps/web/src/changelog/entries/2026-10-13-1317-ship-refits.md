# Refit your ships

- Outdated ships can now be refitted to another design of the same hull instead of scrapped. Use Refit on a fleet's ship row, on a colony's Production panel ("Refit ships in orbit") or with Refit… next to a design in the ship designer.
- A refit happens at one of your colonies in the system the ships orbit; cruisers and bigger need an orbital station there, as for building them.
- It costs twice what the new design costs more than the old one, but at least a quarter of the hull's price. The job joins the colony's build queue, so you can rush it with BC. The panel shows the cost and how many turns it takes.
- While in dock the ship takes no part in anything. It comes back repaired, keeps its crew's experience and rejoins its fleet if that is still there.
- Cancelling a refit, or losing the orbital station a big refit needs, scraps the ship in dock.
- Drives and fuel range still improve on every ship by themselves; armor, shields, computers, weapons and specials now change by refitting.
- Computer empires refit their obsolete warships when it is cheap and quick.
