# Telepathic mind control
- Telepathic empires can now take an enemy colony by mind control after winning its orbit, as in the original. Pick "Mind control" in the orbit window, or the Mind control button in the system panel.
- It needs a cruiser or larger warship in orbit. The colony changes hands whole, with every colonist assimilated at once and no ground battle. A barrier shield doesn't stop it.
- It fails against telepathic races and where the owner has a Telepath officer in that system. The orbit window tells you which reason applies.
- Telepathic computer empires use it too.
