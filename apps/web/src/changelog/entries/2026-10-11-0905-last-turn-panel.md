# Last turn at a glance
- The galaxy map's sidebar now opens with a "Last turn" panel listing what happened: finished production, new technologies, colonies founded or lost, battles, raids, diplomacy and news.
- Click a line to jump there: finished builds open the colony, discoveries open Research, battles open the turn reports, and everything else centers the map on its system.
- Collapse the panel with its header; it remembers your choice.
- Fixed: the Turn report badge and the alert sound after bad news never fired for your own empire.
