# Scan, self-destruct and missile warnings

- Scan in commanded battles, as in the original: see any unit's armor, structure, shields on each facing, beam attack and defense, missile evasion, speed, systems (and which are knocked out) and every weapon with its modifications and shots left.
- Self-destruct: blow up one of your ships. It explodes like a ship whose drive was hit and damages every unit nearby, friend or foe. The button asks you to confirm first.
- Missile warning: the ship whose turn it is lists enemy missiles and squadrons flying at it and when they hit, and warns when your planned move lets them hit sooner.
