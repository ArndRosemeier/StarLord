# Everything says what it does

- Hover or long-press a planet's size, climate, minerals, gravity or special to see what it means for population, industry and research.
- Colony morale breaks down into its causes, with real numbers.
- Buildings, projects, trade goods and ship designs in the build list show their effects, upkeep and this colony's numbers (housing growth, trade goods BC, the next terraforming climate).
- Every technology shows what it does and what it unlocks; hyper-advanced fields explain refinement and cost.
- Government, race abilities and race picks show their effects on the new game screen, in the race builder and on the empire screen.
- Tax buttons preview the BC gained and industry lost.
- Combat stance, doctrine focus and crew experience explain how they change battles.
- Espionage shows the odds of success, framing someone else and losing a spy each turn.
- New game options explain difficulty, galaxy age, tech level, monsters, raiders, the council and random events.
- Leaders list the technologies they bring.
