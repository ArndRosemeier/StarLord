# Veteran crews and original space combat

- Ship crews now gain experience: a little every turn, more at your Space Academies, and a lot from won battles. Regular, Veteran and Elite crews hit harder and dodge better; warlike races rank one level higher.
- The fleet panel shows each ship group's crew rating.
- Battles follow the original rules: the attacker moves first each round, battles last up to 51 rounds and the defender holds the field when time runs out.
- Missiles get faster with better drives and harder to shoot down with each generation; torpedoes do half damage to planets.
- Fighters and bombers fly faster with better drives and get tougher with better armor.
- Planetary defenses lose one installation at random for every 100 to 350 damage that gets through.
- The battlefield is larger, with fleets starting about 30 squares apart.
