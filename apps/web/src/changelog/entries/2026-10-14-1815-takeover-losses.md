# What survives a takeover
- Taking a colony by invasion, mind control or revolt now tears down its Capitol, Marine Barracks, Armor Barracks and Alien Management Center, as in the original. Its build queue and every trained marine and tank are lost; stations, missile bases, batteries, shields and other buildings pass to the new owner.
- A Unification empire also tears down a captured Holo Simulator and Pleasure Dome and gets half their cost back.
- After a won invasion the surviving marines re-board full troop ships and the rest stay on as the garrison; a mind-controlled colony starts with none.
- The orbit window and the turn report name exactly which buildings a takeover destroys.
