# Auto design in the ship designer

- The ship designer has an Auto design button. It offers the same strategies as ship design automation (Balanced, Beam ships, Missile boats, Heavy hulls, Light hulls) and shows the ship each one would make, with its weapons, cost and strength.
- Picking one fits your current design with it. Beam ships, Missile boats and Balanced keep the hull you chose; Heavy and Light hulls pick the hull from your best star dock's industry. Name and style stay, and nothing is saved until you save the design.
