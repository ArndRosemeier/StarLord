# Paid offers, coveted systems and leader fixes

- A computer ruler that is losing a war badly may now offer you something for peace: BC, a technology, a tribute or one of its systems. You get it if you accept.
- A computer ruler that has fought one of your neighbours for a long time and keeps losing ships may offer you BC or a technology to declare war on them. Declining is only declining, even between allies.
- A computer ruler with three strikes against you may demand one of your populous systems near its border before it goes to war. Hand it over, or refuse or ignore the demand and it declares war. This happens from Average difficulty up, never with Honorable rulers and never for your capital.
- Technology trades, demands, gifts and prices only include technologies the other side can use. An advanced government only goes to the empire whose government it advances.
- Tactics administrators now add their skill to the initiative of your ships, stations and planets in their system. It counts in games with the Ship initiative option.
- An improved Spiritual Leader now gives the original's morale: 10, 15, 25, 30, 40 and 45% for levels 1 to 6.
- Raider frigates carry the original's neutronium bomb with no shots in it, so they still never bomb.
