# Escorted colony ships get away
- Colony ships, outpost ships and transports no longer fight in space battles, as in the original: they wait the battle out.
- When your side loses, every ship you have left there withdraws to your nearest colony, so a colony ship with any warship escort always escapes a guarding monster. Alone, it is still lost.
- Ships that pulled out of a battle your side won now stay with their fleet instead of flying home.
- The battle report says what became of the beaten side's remaining ships.
