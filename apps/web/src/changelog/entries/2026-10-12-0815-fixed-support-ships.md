# Colony ships, outpost ships and transports are fixed designs

- As in the original, colony ships, outpost ships and transports can no longer be designed. You build them straight from a colony's build list.
- Each is a frigate hull with your best armor and drive and no weapons. Prices stay 500 BC (colony ship), 100 BC (outpost ship) and 100 BC (transport) before your government's ship cost.
- The ship designer no longer has a Module picker, and support ships no longer appear in its design list.
