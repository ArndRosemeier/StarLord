# Planets screen and range overlays

- New Planets screen, next to Colonies: every planet you know of with its size, climate, minerals, gravity, specials, the most people of your race it holds, its distance and travel time from your nearest colony, whether it is in fuel range, and who owns it.
- Filter it to colony targets, outpost targets or all known planets, only those in fuel range, or by name; click any column header to sort.
- Settle from the list: "Colonize" when a ship is already there, or "Send colony" / "Send outpost" to dispatch your nearest idle ship. Click a row to jump to that system on the galaxy map.
- The galaxy map has two new buttons: "Fuel range" shades everywhere your ships can fly with your current fuel cells, and "Scanners" shows what your colonies and fleets can see. Hover or hold a button for the numbers.
- The Colonies and Fleets lists can now be sorted by any column.
- "Show on map" from another screen now always centres the map on that system.
