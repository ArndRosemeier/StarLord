# Online games with friends

- New on the title screen: Online game. Host a room with your galaxy settings, send friends the invite link (or the six-letter room code), and everyone takes an empire. Empires nobody takes are played by the computer; people without an empire can watch.
- Each player may pick their own race for their empire in the room, including a race they built.
- Turns are simultaneous: End Turn sends your orders, and the bar under the top line shows who is still thinking. The turn runs when everyone is done. Changed your mind? "Change my orders" takes them back while others think.
- You still pick targets, command battles against computer empires and monsters, and choose what to do from orbit. Battles between two players resolve by themselves for now.
- Lost connection or reloaded the page? Open the room link again: the game is rebuilt and you get your empire back. While someone is away, the host can let the computer play their empire (everything but diplomacy) so the game goes on.
- If the host leaves, another player's browser takes over and the game continues. If a player's game ever drifts out of step, it is repaired from the host's copy with a notice.
