# Races screen
- The Diplomacy screen is now the Races screen (R), laid out like the original's: every race you have met, with your relation, treaty, audience button and your spies there.
- Send or recall spies and pick their mission (Espionage, Sabotage, Hide) right on each race's card, with the odds each turn. Your agents at home, agent bonus and security sit below.
- New Report button: the ruler's personality, their agents in your empire, their alliances and wars, and all their technologies, with the ones you don't have highlighted.
- New Ignore button: an ignored race's proposals no longer pop up an audience at the start of your turn. First contact and war declarations still do.
