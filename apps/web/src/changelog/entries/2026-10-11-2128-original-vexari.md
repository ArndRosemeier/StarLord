# The Vexari raid by the original rules

- The Vexari now keep two build budgets: one for raid ships and one for the fleet guarding Vex Prime (3 cruisers, 2 battleships and 7 titans). Once their home fleet is complete, all their points go to raids.
- They buy the smallest ships first. Frigates come only in the first 100 turns after the grace period (83 on Hard, 62 on Impossible, never at the advanced tech level) and destroyers in the first 199 (133 on Hard, 100 on Impossible). On Hard and Impossible their first titan makes every ship 10% cheaper.
- A raid sets off only when the raid ships are worth at least a third of the home fleet plus both unspent budgets. Its chance then grows by 1 in 200 each turn.
- Raids bring up to 6 ships, largest first, and strike a random system with one of your colonies instead of always your most populous one.
- Raid ships go home at the end of the turn and come back on later raids. Only the ships you destroy are gone for good.
- After winning the battle, raiders bomb the colony with their neutronium bombs like any other bombardment. A colony left with nobody is destroyed. They never invade.
- Their ships no longer improve through four eras. Each hull has one fixed design for the whole game, with no shield, Xentronium armor, particle beams, death rays, spatial compressors and a black hole generator on titans. Only the jammer changes with difficulty. They never build doom stars.
- The Vexari Citadel now has 3600 armor and 3600 structure, no shield, an elite crew, and death rays and particle beams that fire all round. The void torpedoes are gone.
- Vex Prime always has at least one titan on guard. Ships lost there are replaced from the raid ships.
