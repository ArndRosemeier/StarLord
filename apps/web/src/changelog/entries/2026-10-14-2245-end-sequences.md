# Every ending gets its own 3D finale
- Each way a game can end now plays its own 3D end sequence before the final score card, with the empire's own ships, colours and homeworld.
- Conquest: your grand fleet sweeps the last enemy wrecks from your homeworld's sky, squadrons ring the planet and the fireworks begin.
- Council: envoys of every surviving empire arrive and salute, and a crown of light forms over your homeworld. The caption shows the real vote count, for example 14 of 21 votes, 14 needed.
- Vex Prime: your fleet breaks the Vexari citadel beside its black hole, and the portal collapses.
- Score: the age ends at dawn over your homeworld while your fleet passes in review, with your lead in points.
- Defeat: the winner's warships stand over your burning homeworld.
- When your empire is wiped out while the others play on, the game now says so with its own ending, naming the empire that struck the last blow. "Watch on" keeps the galaxy open.
- Click, tap or press any key to skip a sequence; it keeps playing quietly behind the score card.
- Debug menu: "Endings" plays any of the five sequences starring your empire.
