# Leaders introduce themselves

- When a leader offers their services, they now step forward in their own window and introduce themselves, as in the original.
- The window shows each skill with its real value at the leader's level, what a colony leader would add to your best system right now, the hiring price, the upkeep and how many leaders of that kind you already have.
- Hire them on the spot, or choose "Not now" and they wait in the hiring queue on the Leaders screen for 30 turns.
