# Monster lairs are worth the fight

- Every system guarded by a monster now always holds a special, as in the original: gold, gems, natives, artifacts, a pirate cache, a waiting hero and so on.
- Star Wyrm lairs hold treasure (pirate cache, gems, natives, a hero or artifacts); Ion Eel lairs hold gold, gems or a splinter colony.
- The guarded planet is raised exactly like the original: Lattice Crystals, Star Hydras and Star Wyrms sit on terran or Gaia worlds; every lair planet is rich or ultra rich and at least medium (large or huge for Crystals, Eels and Hydras).
- The encyclopedia's Monsters page lists what each guard's lair holds.
