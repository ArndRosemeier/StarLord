# Only useful buildings in the build list

- The Gravity Generator is only offered where the planet's gravity actually hurts the colony's people (or its androids). Heavy-world and low-gravity races no longer see it on their home gravity.
- Pollution Processor, Atmospheric Renewer and Core Waste Dumps are hidden for tolerant races, who never pollute, and once Core Waste Dumps stand.
- Holo Simulator and Pleasure Dome are hidden under governments without morale.
- The Alien Management Center is only offered at colonies with conquered or native people.
- Computer players and automation follow the same lists, so they stop wasting production on these.
