# Vexari raids on the original schedule
- The Vexari now stay away for the first 100 turns, 200 if you start pre-warp, and they can come right away if you start advanced.
- After that they gain build points every 25 turns, faster on Hard and Impossible, and buy raid ships with them, from Skiffs up to two Dominators at most.
- A ready raid fleet gets likelier to set off every turn it waits.
- Raids go for the biggest empires and leave the smallest one alone. Lucky empires look smaller to the raiders. On Tutor and Easy a human player's empire looks smaller, and on Hard and Impossible it looks bigger.
