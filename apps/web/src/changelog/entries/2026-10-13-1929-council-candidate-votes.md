# You declare even as a candidate
- Standing for election no longer casts your votes for you: when your seat comes up in the council chamber you back yourselves, your rival, or abstain, like every other ruler.
- Candidates' votes now move relations as in the original: backing your rival while you stand wins their goodwill (+24), voting for yourselves costs some (−12), abstaining a little (−6). Computer candidates feel the same when they vote for themselves.
