# Online games, part two

- Battles between two people in an online game are now fought live: both of you command your own ships, turn by turn, and see each other's moves as they happen. Either side can hand the rest to the computer with Auto-resolve.
- Diplomacy between people: proposals to another person go to them during the turn, and their answer comes back right away.
- Chat with everyone in the room, in the lobby and during the game.
- The host can set a turn timer (1 to 10 minutes). When it runs out, your turn ends with the orders you gave so far; a council vote still waits for you. The timer shows in the online bar.
