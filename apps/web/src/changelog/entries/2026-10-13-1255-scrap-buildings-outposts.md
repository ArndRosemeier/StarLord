# Scrap buildings, outpost gas giants

- Click a building on the Colonies screen to scrap it. You see the refund (half its base cost), the upkeep you save and the effect you lose before you confirm. One building per colony per turn, and never the Capitol.
- Outpost ships can now claim asteroid belts and gas giants, as in the original. Outposts there hold no population but extend your fuel and scanner range and claim the system. Colony ships still need a habitable world.
- A fleet carrying both colony and outpost ships lands the right one: Outpost on asteroids and gas giants, Colonize on habitable planets.
- You don't need an outpost before building an Artificial Planet. If you build one on a body you already hold as an outpost, the outpost stays an outpost on the new world, so the planet picker suggests free bodies first.
- Computer empires now also send outpost ships to asteroid belts and gas giants to extend their reach.
