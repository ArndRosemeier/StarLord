# Encyclopedia brought up to date

- New page "New game, saves & settings": every new-game option with its real effect (each difficulty level, galaxy age and tech level spelled out), saving, autosave, Continue, sound, replay speed, turn flashes and the Hall of Fame.
- "Screens, controls & shortcuts" now lists every screen key, the map overlays, the events pinned to the map and dragging colonists between jobs.
- "How a turn plays out" says when the turn waits for you (commanded battles, orbit choices, council votes) and what opens as a new turn starts.
- New sections on ship refits, breakthrough celebrations, leader introductions and how events are announced.
- The council page shows exactly how computer empires vote and how each vote moves relations.
- The Stars page explains how the galaxy is laid out: homeworld guarantees, where Orion hides, nebulae, wormholes and black holes.
- Fixed: difficulty descriptions showed "+0" for small computer bonuses (now e.g. "+0.25 BC per population"); support ship costs are given in production, not BC.
