# Smarter computer rivals

- Wars between computer empires now end the way they did in the original: after a long war (about 60 turns on Average), not by quick peace offers. Allies now join each other's wars over time, not on the turn the war starts.
- When a computer empire votes in the Galactic Council, it now also counts its mood toward a human candidate. The Diplomacy screen explains this.
- Computer empires keep a real war fleet in peacetime. They build it at their star docks on cruiser and larger hulls as soon as they can afford them, and give their best colony a star dock if it lacks one.
- Computer empires no longer keep idle troop ships in peacetime, or scouts once nothing in reach is left to explore. That leaves command points free for warships.
- A computer colony ship now waits a turn rather than race another empire's settlers that act first for the last free world in a system.
