# Every race builds its own ships

- Each race now has its own ship style across all six hulls: Raptor, Bulwark, Shade, Halo, Hauler, Hive, Lattice, Fang, Spire, Drill, Shard and Manta, plus the Standard warships.
- In the ship designer you can give any design any style, with the Style picker or the arrows on the 3D preview. New designs start in your race's style.
- Every style is painted in its empire's colour, so you can still tell the owners apart in battle and in the system view.
- Custom races choose their ship style in the race designer; a new race starts with a random one.
