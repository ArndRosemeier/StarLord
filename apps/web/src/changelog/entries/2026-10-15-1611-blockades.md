# Blockades like the original
- Any enemy warship left in orbit at the end of a turn now blockades your colonies there, even an unarmed scout, exactly as in the original. Colony, outpost and troop ships don't blockade. Your own ships in the system don't lift it; only driving the enemy off or peace does.
- A blockade starts counting from the next turn, as in the original, so the numbers on the colony screen are the ones you get.
- Colonists moved by freighter into a blockaded system are lost on arrival, as in the original.
- The colony screen shows who blockades you and exactly how much food and industry it costs; blockaded colonies carry a mark in the colony list, and the turn report says when a blockade starts or ends.
