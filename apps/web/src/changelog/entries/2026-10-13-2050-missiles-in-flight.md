# Missiles you can follow

- Missiles, torpedoes and fighter squadrons now stay on screen for their whole flight, in both the 3D and the 2D battle view, in commanded battles and replays.
- Each volley has a glowing head and a short trail, plus a faint dashed line to its target, so you can see what is coming and at whom. In 2D the number of missiles left in the volley is shown next to it.
- When a volley strikes, the hit flies in from where the missiles were, not from the ship that launched them.
