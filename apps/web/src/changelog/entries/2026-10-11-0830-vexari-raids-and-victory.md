# The Vexari: tougher raids and the road to victory
- Vexari raiders now fly real warships with xentronium armor. Their technology grows through four eras and keeps pace with the galaxy's most advanced empire, so raids grow from a pair of cruisers to doom stars.
- A raid that beats a colony's defenses now wrecks buildings as well as killing colonists. Raids come sooner on harder difficulty levels.
- The Dimensional Portal is built at one colony, and only fleets in that system can cross over. A fleet card there shows an "Attack Vex Prime" button. Fleets that don't finish the job come back through your nearest portal.
- Vex Prime is guarded by a home fleet and the Vexari Citadel, an immobile fortress that gets stronger with each era. The Vexari rebuild their fleet before every raid.
- Board Vexari ships and scrap them: each scrapped ship has a 50% chance to teach one of their xenon technologies, which you can learn no other way.
- Wiping out the Vexari at home makes every other empire capitulate, ends the game in your favor and gives the biggest score bonus there is.
- Ion Pulse Cannons do nothing against Vexari ships, while Scout Labs and Galactic Lore captains help against them, as in the original.
