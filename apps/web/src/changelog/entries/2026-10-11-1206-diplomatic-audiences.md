# Face to face with other leaders

- Diplomacy now happens in audiences: click a leader's portrait or Request audience to meet them face to face, with their animated portrait.
- Every race speaks in its own voice when it greets you, accepts, refuses, gives in, threatens or declares war.
- Computer empires answer your proposals and demands on the spot, and the deal holds when the turn ends.
- Other leaders ask for an audience when you first meet, when they declare war and when they bring you a proposal or a demand.
