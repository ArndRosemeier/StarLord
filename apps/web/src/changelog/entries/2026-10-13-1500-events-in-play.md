# Galactic News
- Random events now open a Galactic News report when the new turn starts, so none slips by.
- Events still in play (hyperspace flux, plague, comets, pirates, monsters on their way and more) stay pinned to the top left of the galaxy map, with what they do and when they end.
- Fleets held by hyperspace flux now say so on the map, in the fleet panel and when you give them orders, instead of showing a travel time they can't keep.
- The Fleets screen's travel times now count wormholes, gates and nebulae like the map does.
