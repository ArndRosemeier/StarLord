# Battles are fought over one planet
- Combat now has planet scale, like the original: each battle is fought over one enemy planet, and all your fleets in the system join it.
- When your attacking fleets reach a system with several enemy colonies, you pick which planet to attack, with its defenses, troops and shields shown. You can also pick it ahead with the new Attack button on the planet, or attack nothing this turn.
- Bombing, invasion, mind control and the Stellar Converter strike only the planet the battle was over, so you take at most one planet per system each turn.
- Stations and ground defenses of the other colonies in the system stay out of a battle that isn't over their planet.
- Computer empires pick their target planet by the original's rule: the planet worth most for how surely they beat its defenders.
