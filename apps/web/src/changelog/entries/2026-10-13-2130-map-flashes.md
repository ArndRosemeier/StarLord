# Turn flashes on the galaxy map
- As a turn starts, every star where something happened to you lights up with a bright burst and a short label: ships and buildings finished, colonies founded, battles won or lost, raids, galactic events, starving colonies and newly explored systems, each in its own colour.
- Busy turns ripple across the map one star after another, most important first, and the flashes wait until battle reports and news windows are closed.
- Settings (G) → Galaxy map → Turn flashes: Bursts, Gentle glow or Off. With reduced motion asked for by your system, bursts play as a gentle glow.
