# Fire each weapon group at its own target

- In battles you command, each weapon group now fires on its own, as in the original: pick a target, fire the groups that reach it, then pick another target for the rest.
- A ship can move part of its way, fire, and move on with the movement it has left.
- Every weapon group shows its chance to hit, damage per hit and the damage likely to get through the chosen target's shields, or why it cannot fire (out of arc, out of range, reloading).
- Weapon switches like the original's: Fire, Standby (sits out the next volley) and Off.
- New orders: Turn to face the target, Board an enemy ship your marines can reach, Wait (act with your other ships first) and Done.
