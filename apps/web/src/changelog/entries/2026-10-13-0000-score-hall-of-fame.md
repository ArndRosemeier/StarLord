# Score, Hall of Fame and the Info screen

- The score now follows the original: a point off for every turn, a bonus for galaxy size and the number of empires, points for researched fields and hyper-advanced categories, population, captured population, empires you eliminate, slaying the guardian of Orion, a council win and, biggest of all, destroying the raiders at home. Custom races that leave race picks unspent score up to double.
- Hover your score on the Empire screen to see how it adds up.
- When the game ends you see your final score term by term and the final standings of every empire.
- New Hall of Fame: your best final scores are kept in this browser. Open it from the title screen or after a game.
- New Info screen with standings and history graphs of score, population, colonies, technologies, industry and fleet strength, for you and the empires you have met.
- Hyper-advanced fields now add score for the first level in each category, as in the original.
