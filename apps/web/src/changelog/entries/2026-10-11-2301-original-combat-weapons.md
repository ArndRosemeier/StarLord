# Weapons, damage and bombing as in the original

- Damage now lands the way the original places it. Past the armor each hit goes to a system or the structure, and a system takes only what it can hold before the rest lands again. A weapon hit breaks one weapon at a time, and every point on structure or systems has a 1% chance to kill a marine.
- The Energy Absorber takes a quarter of each hit first (at least 1 point) and fires it back with the ship's next shot. The Damper Field then lets a quarter of the rest through.
- Graviton beams tear half their damage out of the structure twice, before armor.
- Range is measured between the nearest squares of two ships. Frigates fill one square, destroyers and cruisers 2×2, larger ships and planets 3×3.
- Battles no longer end early because nothing happened for two turns. They go on until one side is gone or time runs out.
- Ships carry their bombs into battle and drop them on an enemy planet from 3 squares (12 with Transporters). Once a planet's installations are gone, further damage hits the colony itself.
- Bombarding a colony after a battle now uses every weapon in the fleet for 10 combat turns, not just bombs.
- Fighters make one pass a turn: interceptors 4, heavy fighters 2, bombers 1. They carry your best point-defense-capable beam and aim with 50 plus your best computer.
- Point defense and Anti-Missile Rockets only fire at missiles that would get through the shields. Each rocket now has a 64% chance.
- The Gyro Destabilizer does 1-4 damage per hull size step straight into the structure.
- Pulsars and Spatial Compressors go off once an enemy is within 3 squares and hit every ship around, friend or foe. The compressor's damage goes straight into the structure.
- A Stasis Field holds its ship until it is let go. A Black Hole Generator can catch stations and destroys its prey at the end of the second turn after.
- Hyper-X Capacitors and Fast Missile Racks fire twice after a turn's rest, once otherwise. A Phasing Cloak keeps its ship out of phase for 10 of its turns while it holds fire, then works as a normal cloak.
- Ion weapons can no longer be aimed at raiders or monsters. Special weapons other than the Stellar Converter can't target planets.
- Repair units and cybernetic crews now add up their repair. They mend all systems, broken weapons included.
- A telepath keeps a captured ship even if its side loses the battle.
- Conquered colonies now yield up to 4 techs, with odds from their buildings and population.
- Support ships now cost what they did in the original: colony ships 500, outpost ships and transports 100 each, before government discounts.
