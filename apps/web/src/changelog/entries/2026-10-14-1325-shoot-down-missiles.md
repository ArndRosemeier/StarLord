# Shoot down missiles
- Every beam weapon except heavy mounts can now fire at missile volleys and fighter squadrons in flight, as in the original. Anti-missile rockets can be fired at missiles before they arrive too. Torpedoes still cannot be shot down.
- In battles you command, pick a volley from the "Incoming" warning ("Shoot it down") or from the "In flight" list under the target. Each weapon group shows its hit chance and how many missiles it should down.
- Each beam hit downs at most one missile; weaker hits add up on the same missile.
- Computer ships now shoot at a volley when downing it saves more damage than firing at their target.
- Point-defense mounts still fire on their own, inside their arc, just before missiles strike.
