# Council votes like the original, and empires fight

- Computer empires now vote at the Galactic Council by the original game's rule: attitude, trust and reputation plus points for pacts, treaties, charisma, Xeno Psychology and holding Orion. They always back an ally, never an enemy, and abstain unless exactly one candidate appeals to them. The Diplomacy screen shows each empire's current odds of backing either candidate.
- Computer empires now go to war over a crowded galaxy, not only out of dislike. As in the original, a signed treaty stops them declaring war on that partner for a while, longer for Honorable and Pacifistic rulers (shown in each ruler's temperament).
- Fixed: computer fleets no longer shuttle back and forth near Orion's guardian instead of attacking, and a strike force now invades enemy colonies in its own home system.
