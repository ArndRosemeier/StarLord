# Every rival has its own character
- Computer rulers now roll their temperament from their own race's leanings, like in the original: the hive insectoids are nearly always xenophobic, the scholars mostly pacifistic, the feline warriors mostly ruthless. Harder difficulty levels tilt every race toward its hostile side.
- Each computer ruler also has an objective (Diplomat, Militarist, Expansionist, Technologist, Industrialist or Ecologist) and a ship doctrine (a favourite weapon and a ship specialty such as shields, armor, stealth or boarding), weighted by its race's strengths.
- Objectives and doctrines shape what they research first, how much they put into science, how bravely their fleets fight, and which weapons and special systems their warships carry.
- The Diplomacy screen shows every ruler's objective and doctrine next to its temperament; hover them to see exactly what they do. The guide's Diplomacy page lists which temperaments each race can have.
