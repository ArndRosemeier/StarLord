# Fleets travel together

- Fleets you send to the same star from the same place now travel as one group and arrive together. Before, a fleet with a Navigator captain raced ahead and left the others, such as your colony ships, behind.
- As in the original, the group's best Navigator speeds up every ship in it and steers all of them through nebulae.
- The fleet panel shows how many other fleets travel with a fleet and their shared speed. Arrival times for several selected fleets count the group pace.
