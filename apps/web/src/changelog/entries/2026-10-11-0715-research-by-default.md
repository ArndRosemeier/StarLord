# Research from turn one
- New colonies now split their people evenly between industry and research after feeding themselves, so your empire advances even if you never touch the job sliders. Newly grown colonists keep the split even.
- Ending a turn with no research target now warns you and offers to open the Research screen.
- A colony with an empty build queue turns its industry into Trade Goods. The galaxy map's side panel now lists every such colony; click one to open it and give it something to build.
