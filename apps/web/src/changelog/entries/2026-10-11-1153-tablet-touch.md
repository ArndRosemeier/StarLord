# Play on a tablet

- Pinch to zoom and drag with one or two fingers to pan the galaxy map. The 3D system view, battles and ship previews orbit with a finger, zoom with a pinch and reset with a double tap.
- Hold a finger on any button, number or control to see its tooltip, including every part in the ship designer. Lifting the finger afterwards does not press it.
- Hold a fleet on the map to add it to the selection, or switch on "Select several" at the bottom of the map so every tap adds or removes a fleet.
- Bigger buttons and fields on touch screens, a top bar that fits on one line at tablet widths, and a ✎ button to rename fleets.
- The encyclopedia's Controls page explains the touch controls.
