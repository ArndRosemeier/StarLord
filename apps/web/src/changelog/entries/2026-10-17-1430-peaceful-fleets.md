# Peaceful fleets stay peaceful

- Moving ships into a star system of an empire you are not at war with never starts a battle or a war, as in the original. Only a declared war makes ships fight.
- As in the original, a big armed fleet parked at a computer empire's large colony (5+ population) still sours its ruler each turn unless you are allied, which can lead to warnings and, in the end, war. On Tutor and Easy this no longer happens, matching the original.
