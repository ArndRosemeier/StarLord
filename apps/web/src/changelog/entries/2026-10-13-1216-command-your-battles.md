# Command your battles

- When you end the turn, it now stops at every battle you take part in. Choose to command it yourself or auto-resolve it.
- Commanding works like the original: on each turn of your ships, stations and armed planets (your missile base too), click the field to move inside the blue ring, pick an enemy to fire at (orange rings are in reach, grey ones are not), then press Fire or End turn.
- Each ship can also retreat or let the computer play its turn, and "Auto-resolve the rest" hands the remaining fight to your combat doctrine at any time.
- Prefer the old way? Untick "Let me command my battles" under Game or on the Fleets screen, and every battle resolves by itself with its replay after the turn.
- Battles you commanded don't pop up again after the turn; they stay in the turn report for replay.
