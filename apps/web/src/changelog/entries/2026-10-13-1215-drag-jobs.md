# Drag colonists between jobs

- In the colony panel you can now drag farmers, workers and scientists from one job row to another, with the mouse or a finger.
- Grab further left to move more people: grabbing the 4th worker from the right moves 4 workers.
- While you drag, a card shows the colony's food, industry and research after the move, with the change from now.
- Natives stay on the farm and can't be dragged. The − and + buttons still work as before.
