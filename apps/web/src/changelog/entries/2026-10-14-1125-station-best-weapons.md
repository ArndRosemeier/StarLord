# Stations carry your best beam
- Star bases, battlestations, star fortresses and ground batteries now mount the beam with the highest damage you know, as in the original. Before, they took the beam with the most damage per space, so a Gauss Cannon or Phasor could be fitted instead of your newer Plasma Cannon or Disruptor.
- Point-defense beams on stations are now picked by the original's rule too.
- Hover a station on the Colonies screen to see exactly what it fights with right now: weapons, armor, shield, beam attack and defense, marines and free specials.
- A colony's planet now always appears in battles at that colony, right beside its station, as in the original, even without ground batteries or a missile base. Enemy fleets that did not come to attack the planet still only shoot at the guns firing on them.
