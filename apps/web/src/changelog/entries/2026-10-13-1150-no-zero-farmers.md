# No more farmers on barren worlds

- New colonies on worlds where farming grows no food (barren, radiated, toxic, inferno and the like) now put their colonists to work in industry and research instead of farming. Their food comes from your other colonies by freighter.
- New colonists, the computer empires and every Jobs automation strategy never send anyone to farm where a farmer would grow nothing.
- The colony panel shows how much food one more farmer would grow, or warns that farming grows no food there.
