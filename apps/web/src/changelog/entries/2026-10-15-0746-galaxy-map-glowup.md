# StarLord 1.000: the galaxy map glows up
Version: 1.000
- Version 1.000! From here every build counts on: 1.001, 1.002 and so on.
- Empires now show their territory: every colonised system claims about 2.2 parsecs of space around it, neighbouring claims flow together into one realm, and where two empires meet the stronger pull wins. Each realm gets a soft fill in its colour with a glowing border line that stays crisp at any zoom.
- Nebulae are now drawn as drifting violet and rose gas clouds instead of plain glows, so you can see at a glance where ships slow down and shields fail.
- Stars got a makeover: explored stars wear a slowly turning corona, and their size shows their class, from big blue giants down to small brown dwarfs.
- Names of colonised systems are tinted in their owner's colour, and colony rings glow softly.
- Fleets under way leave an engine trail, their routes glow, and a slow ripple marks where they will arrive.
- The selected system gets a turning four-bracket marker, a far star field drifts slower than the map for depth, and the screen edges darken slightly to frame the galaxy.
- With reduced motion switched on in your system settings, everything holds still as before.
