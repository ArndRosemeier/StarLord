# Diplomacy shows what it does

- Every treaty you hold now says what it does for you right now: BC and research a turn from trade and research treaties, when they reach full strength, tribute paid or received, how long a peace treaty protects you, and what a pact or alliance gives.
- In an audience, every proposal, demand, gift and treaty-ending option explains its effect with the game's real numbers before you pick it.
- Declaring war lists what it sets off: the attitude cost, treaties that end, and which allies will join the war against you.
- Proposals made to you say what accepting and refusing will mean.
- Each empire's attitude toward you can be opened to see exactly why they feel the way they do and where it is heading.
