# 3D ships in the ship designer

- The ship designer now shows your hull as the same 3D model it flies in battle, in your empire's colour. Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset.
- The design list shows a 3D portrait of each design's hull instead of the old flat ship pictures.
- All six hull classes have new, more detailed models with layered decks, gun turrets, bridges and glowing engines, in battles and the system view too.
