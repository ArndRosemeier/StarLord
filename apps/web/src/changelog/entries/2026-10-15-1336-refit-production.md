# Refits are planetary production

- Every colony's production screen now has a **Refit ships in orbit** button whenever ships of yours are in its system, as in the original.
- A refit is a normal build item: the ship is gone from its fleet while it waits and is built, and comes back only when the refit is finished.
- Move refits around the queue like anything else. Taking one out of the queue, or losing the colony, brings the ship back unchanged instead of scrapping it.
