# Colonies follow the original formulas
- Population growth, maximum population, pollution, taxes, morale and buying now use the exact formulas from the original game.
- Ocean, tundra and desert worlds hold as few people as hostile ones; swamp, arid, terran and gaia worlds hold 40%, 60%, 80% and 100% of a planet's full size. Tolerant races add 25% instead of treating every world as terran.
- Each colonist pays 1 BC in tax, and the tax rate turns production into BC one to one. Morale, government, gravity and leader bonuses now add up instead of multiplying each other, and building bonuses are no longer scaled.
- Buying is pricier before an item is half done: 2 BC per missing point in the last half, 5 BC from 10% to half and 10 BC below 10%. Upgrading a station or planetary shield costs only the difference.
- Starving colonies lose 50k people per missing food each turn instead of a whole colonist, workers and scientists first. Housing, population booms and plagues work through growth like in the original.
- Computer players get the original's difficulty bonuses to output, growth and taxes.
